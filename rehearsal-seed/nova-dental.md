**Scenario**: SA is pitching **Relay for Clinics** (no-show recovery + waitlist auto-fill) to Nova Dental Clinic (novadentalclinic.ae), a 3-location dental practice in Dubai (Jumeirah, Al Barsha, Motor City). 30-min pitch. Three people in the room. SA has uploaded three product docs the personas were sent 48 hours before the meeting:

- `Relay Product Demo.mp4` (11:42)
- `Case Studies — Dental.pdf` (Bright Smile Toronto, PearlWorks Austin, Kensington Dental London)
- `Pricing One-Pager.pdf` (Starter AED 2,400/mo per location, Growth AED 4,800/mo, Enterprise custom)

Nova Dental context Astra has pulled from public sources: ~4,200 active patient records across three locations, ~9,000 booked visits/month, publicly quoted "under 5% no-show rate" (Dr. Rashid, Gulf News, March 2026), 32 chairs total, uses **Dentrix Ascend** for practice management (spotted in a Fatima Hassan LinkedIn comment on a Dentrix user-group post).

### 5. Coaching brief

**Unchallenged SA claims**:
- SA said "30 to 40% of previously lost revenue" and never defined the denominator. Aisha caught the gross vs net question, but SA didn't clarify whether "previously lost" means slots that went empty OR slots that were rebooked internally within the same week (which most PMS systems already handle for free). That distinction cuts the recovery number in half at most Dubai clinics.
- SA claimed "6 to 9% on total booked visits" as a waitlist-conversion lift with no confidence interval and no baseline definition. Aisha will find this in the raw data.
- SA said "at your no-show rate the strongest ROI lever is probably waitlist conversion" — she let this slide but it contradicts the entire opening pitch which was framed around no-show recovery. In the real meeting she will use this contradiction against you.

**Under-addressed objections**:
- Objection C ("where does the AED land if we're not cutting Fatima") got a one-word answer ("more visits"). Aisha needed a walkthrough: incremental visits × ticket × margin, arriving at a monthly AED number that maps against AED 14,400 spend. She asked for the arithmetic. She got a category.
- The Kensington FX/insurance-mix question is real. If you handwave it, expect her to email your CEO.

**Suggested rewrite**:
Instead of: "*at your no-show rate the strongest ROI lever is probably waitlist conversion, so more visits. Maybe 6 to 9%.*"
Try: "*At your baseline, no-show recovery is a small pool. The bigger lever for Nova is waitlist conversion — turning a 3-week wait into a same-week appointment. On our four dental clients under 6% no-show, waitlist conversion added between 7.1 and 8.4% to total booked visits in months 2 through 6. At Nova's 9,000 visits a month and AED 380 ticket, the mid-point puts you around AED 265K in incremental revenue against AED 14,400 in spend. Payback lands inside month 2.*"

**Voice/tone mirror**:
Match her cadence. Short sentences. Numbers before adjectives. Don't fill her silences — she uses them to make you commit. When she asks "which case studies," don't list three cities. Name one, and ask which one she wants to dig into.
